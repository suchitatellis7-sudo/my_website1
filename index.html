Plan Your Dandeli Trip

Best season Oct–Mar for pleasant weather; water activities vary by dam release. Always check slots for rafting.

Quick facts

  • Where: Dandeli, Uttara Kannada, Karnataka, India
  • Known for: Kali River rafting, Kali Tiger Reserve, hornbills, Syntheri Rocks, Kavala Caves
  • Best time: Oct–Mar (cool/pleasant). Rafting depends on water release; often good post‑monsoon.
  • Nearest hubs: Hubballi (HBX ~75 km), Belagavi (IXG ~105 km), Goa (150–180 km)
Tip: Carry government ID, closed‑toe footwear for rafting, and follow forest rules. Plastic‑free travel is encouraged.

Map

Getting there

By air via Hubballi or Belagavi, then taxi/bus. By rail via Alnavar/Londa/Hubballi. Good road links from Goa and North Karnataka.

FAQ & Trip Tips

Quick answers to common questions for first‑time visitors.

When is the best time to visit Dandeli?

October to March usually offers pleasant weather. Rafting is subject to water release from the dam and is often best post‑monsoon; availability can vary.

Do I need a permit for safaris?

Safaris inside Kali Tiger Reserve run under the Forest Department with limited seats and fixed routes. We can help you with the latest process and availability.

Can beginners do rafting? Is it safe?

Yes, the usual run has Grade II–III rapids and is operated with helmets, life jackets and trained river guides. Listen to your guide and wear proper footwear.

Where should I keep photos and media for this site?
